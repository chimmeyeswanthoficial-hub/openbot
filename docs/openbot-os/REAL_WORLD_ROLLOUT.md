# Real-world launch, one-month trial, then six-month expansion

**Owner's requested sequence:** finish the engineering and readiness work, launch
an explicitly authorized real-world scope, test it for one month, then run a
further six months of real-world evaluation while improving it step by step.
No demo, screenshot, simulated signer, automated test, clock jump, elapsed idle
calendar time or invented user is credited as a real pilot observation.

**Current state:** not launched; trial not started; real accepted observed days
**0** in both phases. No launch date or fabricated team/on-call is assigned.
The plan preserves all R01–R13 requirements. It does not reinterpret the broader
PC/provider/IDE/social/native vision as a docs-only product.

## Stage 0 — engineering and safe operational readiness

Complete and independently review the promised launch scope before inviting real
users. Internal/local engineering tests can proceed now, but do not count as pilot.

Required before controlled real-world use:

1. Exact supported workflows/platforms, source SHA, reproducible build and runtime
   dependency/SBOM/license notices. The first-party MIT file is not the whole audit.
2. Authenticated history + compatible agent/provider adapter + mandatory policy/
   pre-effect audit/approval/cost broker. No fake assistant responses or hidden
   browser authority. Provider keys stay in a protected owner-side vault.
3. Actual per-bot VM/microVM and resource/egress/secret/cross-bot/kill/recovery proof
   whenever PCs/IDE work are included. Folders/containers are not substitutes.
4. Actual approved Windows/Android/iOS launches, lifecycle, accessibility, pairing,
   signed update/rollback/uninstall on the platforms promised for launch. Current
   historical EXE/APK are not rebuilt OpenBot OS releases.
5. Official social/account permissions, exact-action consent and read-back if social
   is promised. Private uploads/posts are still real external effects.
6. Independent security/privacy/supply-chain review, accepted critical remediations,
   genuine backup/restore/rollback/alerts drill and named consenting on-call/support.
7. Independently pre-freeze at least 20 held-out real representative tasks, baseline,
   criteria, budgets/limits and stop rules outside bot-writable source. Compare on
   the same permitted model/config/hardware/tools, not unit counts or marketing.
8. Owner accepts the exact private launch cohort, account/data consent, max exposure,
   permitted actions, retention, support and stop mechanism. No API keys in chat.

Start with a **controlled private real-world launch**, not an unauthorized public
release. Real consenting operators, real useful work and genuinely measured outcomes
are necessary; a publicly reachable planning preview does not meet this definition.
This resolves "launch then test" without skipping the standing human public-release
and security gates. No provider spending, external account login or host change is
authorized merely by this plan.

## Phase 1 — first month: at least 30 consecutive observed real days

- Starts only at the actual authorized rollout, not this document's date or a Git
  timestamp. Freeze the phase-1 build/config/criteria/allowed effects first.
- Use consented real work, initially human-supervised and within explicit low caps.
  Keep developer accounts, synthetic fixtures and real users/evidence separate.
- Every day capture a reviewed evidence record: build/config/host identity, useful
  task outcomes and failures, authority/approval/denial events, actual provider/VM
  usage reconciliation, crash/reconnect/data loss, recovery, human corrections,
  support burden, accessibility issues and incidents. Evidence references must
  resolve to protected retained raw records, not only a self-written JSON flag.
- Gaps, outages, missing cost data and unobserved days remain visible. Do not backfill
  signed observations or count retries/unit tests as separate successful days.
- A calendar month does not remove the standing **30 consecutive observed-day minimum**.
  If a shorter month or any gap fails that minimum, extend the phase. Elapsed time
  alone does not pass it.
- Weekly review: root-cause real failures, fix one bounded area, rerun appropriate
  regressions/security checks, version the change, canary and verify rollback.
  A material authority/model/provider/migration change needs re-approval and affected
  evidence re-baselining; never pool old/new builds into a false unchanged cohort.

**Phase-1 exit:** independent evidence review and owner go/no-go for expansion,
with agreed success/cost/reliability thresholds met and no unaccepted critical issue.
No automatic promotion or blanket autonomy after day 30.

## Phase 2 — six additional calendar months of observed real-world use

Begins **after** phase-1 acceptance, under a new explicit scope/cohort/cost/on-call
approval. It is not six months including phase 1, and not simply "180 synthetic days".

- Freeze the exact start/end dates at that actual phase transition. Six calendar
  months uses calendar-month arithmetic; month-end dates clamp to the destination
  month's final day. The independent reviewer approves the date convention. Actual
  calendar duration can exceed the current ledger's 180-day limit.
- Expand cohort/workflows gradually, one provider/VM/IDE/social/native track at a
  time, only after its specific authority, security and real-device gates.
- Continue genuine daily observations, weekly reliability/incident review and
  monthly independent quality/cost/privacy/accessibility/capacity review. Record
  build, model, prompt, policy, dependency and migration changes explicitly.
- Measure task usefulness, failure rate/severity, unauthorized/uncertain actions,
  cost versus reconciled usage, human intervention, latency, device reliability,
  backup restores, update/rollback, abuse handling and user-reported harm. Report
  denominators, failed tasks, uncertainty and gaps, not only selected successes.
- Keep learned skills/memory behind consent, provenance, review and rollback. No
  self-modification of budgets/approval policy/evaluator/release keys. Do not train
  on private user data or protected evaluation tasks by default.

**Phase-2 exit:** qualified independent six-month report with raw evidence, resolved
critical blockers and explicit owner decision for the next release/expansion scope.
No guarantee of profit, zero incidents or universal superiority.

## Immediate stop and recovery rules — both phases

Pause new effects on suspected secret/tenant exposure, unauthorized action,
unknown or over-limit spending, inability to stop a workload, data corruption/loss,
compromised update or missing authority/audit. Notify the real on-call owner; preserve
private evidence, revoke affected grants and reconcile uncertain effects **before
retrying**. Perform reviewed rollback/restore and incident review before resumption.
A consent withdrawal follows the tested export/deletion/backup policy; deletion is
not silently equated with secure erase of immutable audits or provider records.

## Reuse existing evaluation machinery — do not counterfeit an upgrade

Existing `evaluation_controls.Assessment` already rejects fixture-mode acceptance,
requires protected independently signed evaluation/current-day observations and
keeps `pilot_gate_accepted`/`release_authorized` false. Reuse its useful protections;
no second weaker pilot counter is being introduced.

**Known engineering blocker:** Assessment v1 has one frozen candidate/policy and a
**180-day maximum**, not this requested sequential one-month + six-calendar-month
release process. Do not silently raise its limit, reinterpret 180 days as six
calendar months, reuse a grant for a new build, flip fixture mode or self-award
acceptance. A versioned phase-transition/rolling-build/evidence-continuity extension
with proper migration, signed reapproval, retained gaps and negative tests must be
reviewed before phase 2 can be admitted. Existing prototype policy workflows also
cover only bounded QA/media, not every promised product workflow.

`docs/openbot-os/REAL_WORLD_PILOT_STATUS.json` is an honest **unstarted plan/status**,
not an operating ledger or approval. No genuine observation is generated here.
Independent reviewers, real users, accounts, deployed hosts, native devices and
months of observation cannot be manufactured during a chat session.
