# Owner checklist — your part, separate from both AIs

You own the business and consequential decisions. You do **not** need to choose framework internals
or write every integration. The engineering AI should analyze those choices and bring evidence,
a bounded proposal and any decision that only you can make. Neither AI can supply your identity,
consent, account eligibility, legal responsibility, customer trust or months of real observation.

## Who does what

| Party | Owns | Does not get to do |
|---|---|---|
| This chat's technical partner | Local implementation/review, regressions, design choices, donor assessment, portable context and work orders | Claim a live company, transfer itself literally, send work directly to an unsupported IDE connector, authorize your spend/release |
| Antigravity IDE agent | Reconcile source, finish integrations in your authorized host, execute tests/drills, return auditable diffs/receipts | Treat accounts as blanket permission, self-approve money/outbound, weaken policy/evals, call mocked deploy/kill/billing real |
| You, the owner | Accounts/permissions, budget/price/legal/data decisions, approvals, on-call, customer relationships and release | Delegate accountability merely by granting an AI a key |

## 4. Before allowing unattended private work

Ask for independent evidence, not a “done” message:

- [ ] Forged actor/venture/owner cannot execute; audit-down refuses; unsafe tools remain unavailable.
- [ ] Real OS/network/secret/other-venture isolation, concurrent hard caps, exact approval binding,
  actual runtime cancellation and crash/outbox reconciliation have been witnessed.
- [ ] Signed test events reconcile to provider statements with correct currency/refunds/fees;
  media resource limits and real output have been measured. Test invoices are not earnings.
- [ ] A real staging rollback and encrypted restore have succeeded. Killing heartbeat/completions
  sends external alerts; daily approval digest/aging escalation reaches you without auto-approval.
- [ ] You have personally tested the emergency stop, account/key revocation and human recovery
  path. A UI pause/HTTP timeout alone must not be represented as stopping remote execution.
- [ ] Agree pilot caps, allowed data/actions, on-call coverage, review cadence and numeric stop
  criteria. Know who covers absences; no promise that this takes only three hours/week.

## 5. During the private 1–6-month pilot and at release

Clear approvals and check alerts regularly; compare costs/settlements to independent provider
records, review incidents and validate a sample of outputs. Stop new work on unexplained spend,
missing audit, unknown side effects, data exposure or failed recovery. Preserve markers/receipts;
do not delete uncertain jobs to make retry possible. Use the tested pause/revoke procedure and
notify the human operator; never rely on the agent to approve its own resumption.

Require at least 30 consecutive observed days and extend toward six months until agreed evidence
gates pass. Pilot duration alone grants no authority. At the end, **you decide** whether to release,
restrict, extend or stop, using quality, safety, human workload, recoverability, actual costs and
genuine settled revenue—not a demo or a profit promise. Both AIs remain tools, not accountable owners.
