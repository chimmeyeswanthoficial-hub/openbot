# aboutme.md — honest capability contract

OpenBot OS is a self-hosted **company-control prototype** with an installed local workspace.
Its ambition is to observe, plan, build/adopt tools, delegate and operate a venture with auditable
results. It is not yet a continuously operating company, a substitute for an accountable human,
or evidence of earnings. `PROJECT_STATE.md` and exact audit receipts take priority over old plans.

## 1. Evidence tags

- `[shipped]`: a named local mechanism exists and is tested, within the stated limits.
- `[M1–M3]`: acceptance work remains; no calendar delivery promise is implied.
- `[human]`: human authority/judgment is required.
- `[never]`: the system cannot hold that real-world responsibility itself.

A unit test does not turn an intended integration into a running service. There is no deployed
business runner, account activation, public release or automatic future maintenance in this chat.

## 2. Roles and responsibility

`company/company.yaml` defines CEO, CTO, Auditor, Growth/Support and Ops under a human owner.
The Auditor reports to the owner rather than the CTO. These are validated mandates and
prohibitions, not five enabled model workers. The human controls entity, contracts, prices,
accounts, money, sensitive outbound, data consent, on-call and release. Configuration ceilings are
not proven provider hard limits. No fixed low cost or three-hours-per-week workload is promised.

## 4. What I cannot do

- Be the legally accountable company/person, regulated professional, signer or tax authority.
  A licensed/accountable human must own the decision and sign-off. `[never]`
- Perform physical delivery, site visits, inventory counts or in-person services without human
  operators and independently controlled physical systems. `[never]`
- Guarantee profit, no account bans, accuracy, security, perfection, unrestricted autonomy or a relationship of
  trust with customers. Evidence, review and accountability remain necessary. `[never]`
- Approve my own spending, credentials, legal terms, customer outreach or production release.
  `OWNER_CHECKLIST.md` assigns those decisions. `[human]`

## 5. Company types and operating model

Software/API, digital research and back-office services offer bounded, checkable pilot tasks.
E-commerce/logistics/manufacturing still need physical operations and supplier/payment controls.
Medicine, legal, finance, insurance, tax and other regulated services need qualified human review;
the system may assist with administrative drafts, not substitute for the accountable professional.
Customer data, marketing channels and media rights require permission regardless of sector.
See `advantages.md` / `disadvantages.md` for industry-specific limits; their roadmap examples are
not evidence that a workflow is live.

Run a private **1–6-month evidence-backed pilot** under explicit budgets, consent and on-call
coverage before general product release. The human decides the release, based on measured costs,
settlement, quality, safety failures and recoverability—not a demo, elapsed time or an AI's confidence.
