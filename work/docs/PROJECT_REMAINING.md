## Owner part / release bar

`OWNER_CHECKLIST.md` contains the non-delegable decisions and a minimal first handoff message.
There is no reason to buy every donor's services or enable all integrations first. Local development
needs no model, Intelligence or payment account. Account availability and terms are human checks.

Before a private pilot: independently witness auth/authority denials, hostile sandbox/egress probes,
concurrent hard limits, exact approval/replay, cancel/restart uncertainty, canonical accounting,
bounded media resource use, backup restore, real rollback and external missed-heartbeat alerts.
After at least one and up to six months of agreed evidence, the human decides product release.
No guaranteed profit, no universal-company autonomy, no time-only release rule.

Use/extend `.claude/skills/`; show red before fixing behavior; preserve failed traces; rerun whole
suite and independent restore after a source freeze. Keep source/locks/build/runtime/provider
claims separate. Record exact rows/digests/commands and what was not exercised. Update the brain's
predictions, not just status. Never weaken a check to pass, self-approve a sensitive act, delete
uncertain request markers, expose raw hosts or silently purge donor/history files to fit the cap.
